
Audit Objective :-

The audit objective in the approved 2026 audit plan was:

• To assess the governance and control effectiveness of agent network management including sales steering and target setting; sales support and administration; remuneration and incentive schemes; and sales excellence, recruitment and training. The audit will focus on Independent Financial Advisor (IFA) management.

The audit will be performed as a catalogue audit.

The audit object, per the Master Audit Universe is Agent Network Management including Contact Management.

The audit work will encompass interviews with appropriate management and other staff members, as well as the review of processes and respective key documentation to assess the design and effectiveness of in scope processes and internal controls. The performance or outcome of the respective key processes and controls will be verified by means of operating effectiveness or substantive testing.

Risk :- Non-existing, Incomplete, or Non-compliant Policies, Standards and Functional Rules.

Control :- Gov 3b_Operational Structure Documentation and Procedural Knowledge / Content Expertise.

Control description:- Up-to-date documentation of operational structure (e.g., Corporate Rules/Policies, Adonis/Process descriptions, etc.)

1. Restated Risk
The risk addresses a governance failure in which the policies, standards, and functional rules governing agent network management — specifically Independent Financial Advisor (IFA) management — either do not exist, are materially incomplete, or do not comply with applicable group-level or regulatory requirements. The failure pathway is a normative-framework gap: absent or deficient governing documents leave the audited function without enforceable operational boundaries, creating exposure to non-compliant practices, inconsistent process execution, and unmitigated operational risk across the in-scope areas (sales steering, target setting, sales support, administration, remuneration/incentive schemes, recruitment, and training).

2. Restated Control Description
The control description as provided states: "Up-to-date documentation of operational structure (e.g., Corporate Rules/Policies, Adonis/Process descriptions, etc.)." This asserts the existence of current documentation. No actor is named; no action verb describes a verification, review, or maintenance activity; no frequency or trigger is stated; and no evidence type is identified.

It is noted that the full Gov_3b definition in the Allianz Standard for Internal Audit (SAM) V8.0, Annex A, reads: "Up-to-date documentation of operational structure (e.g., Corporate Rules/Policies, Adonis/Process descriptions, etc.) and proper communication of the structure to all affected staff (including training sessions)." The control description provided omits the communication and training element entirely.

3. Mitigation Assessment
Threshold Test determination: The control description is a condition statement, not a control activity. It asserts a desired end-state — that documentation is "up-to-date" — without identifying an actor performing a defined action at a defined trigger or frequency. There is no action verb (e.g., "reviews," "validates," "updates," "approves") and no named or implied performer. Because the description is a condition statement with no actor and no action, the verdict ceiling for this assessment is Does Not Mitigate, regardless of topical overlap with the risk.

Formal rating: Does Not Mitigate.

Failure-pathway mapping: The risk identifies three distinct failure modes: (a) non-existence of policies/standards/functional rules, (b) incompleteness of those documents, and (c) non-compliance of those documents with applicable requirements. A control that mitigates this risk would need to include a mechanism that periodically verifies that the required document set exists, is complete in its coverage of the audited function's processes, and conforms to group-level and regulatory requirements — and that triggers corrective action when gaps are found.

The control description addresses none of these failure modes through an operational mechanism. It asserts that documentation is "up-to-date" but provides no process by which currency, completeness, or compliance is verified. A condition statement cannot interrupt, detect, or prevent the risk's failure pathway; it can only describe the outcome that a properly functioning control would produce.

Unaddressed elements of the risk:

No mechanism to verify existence of the required policy/standard/functional rule set.
No mechanism to assess completeness relative to the scope of agent network management/IFA management.
No mechanism to assess compliance with group-level requirements or regulatory mandates.
The communication/training element from the full SAM Gov_3b definition is absent from the description provided, leaving no coverage of the risk that staff operate without awareness of governing documents even if those documents exist.
Scope-mismatch observation: The description references "operational structure" documentation generically (Corporate Rules/Policies, Adonis/Process descriptions) but does not bound this to the specific audited function — agent network management or IFA management. This creates ambiguity about whether the control is scoped to the process where the risk materializes.

4. Control Type Consideration
The description cannot be classified as preventive or detective because no operational mechanism is described. A condition statement has no timing of action, and therefore no exposure window to evaluate. If the control were operationalized as a periodic review of documentation currency and completeness, it would function as a detective control — identifying gaps after they arise rather than preventing them from occurring. Given that the risk concerns the adequacy of the governing framework itself, a detective mechanism (periodic review and gap identification) would be appropriate, provided it operates at a frequency that limits the window of exposure. A purely preventive mechanism (e.g., mandatory approval gates before any policy can lapse) could supplement but is not strictly required. However, this analysis is moot until the description is rewritten to describe an actual control activity.

5. Specificity Assessment (4W+1H)
Per the NFRM Functional Rule, Section C.IV.2: a control description should specify what is monitored, why the control is necessary, who performs or owns it, when and how frequently it is carried out, and how it is carried out including the evidence generated.

What (subject monitored): Partially Stated. "Documentation of operational structure" is named with examples (Corporate Rules/Policies, Adonis/Process descriptions), but it is not bounded to the specific audited process (agent network management / IFA management). The scope of what documentation is "monitored" is open-ended.
Why (linkage to the risk): Absent. No stated connection to the risk of non-existing, incomplete, or non-compliant policies/standards/functional rules. The description does not explain why maintaining this documentation addresses the failure modes in the risk.
Who (actor/owner): Absent. No individual, role, function, or committee is identified as responsible for ensuring the documentation is up-to-date.
When (frequency/trigger): Absent. "Up-to-date" implies currency but states no review cycle, trigger event (e.g., regulatory change, annual review), or deadline.
How (method and evidence generated): Absent. No verification method is described (e.g., gap analysis against a policy register, completeness check against a regulatory requirements list). No evidence type is named — there is no reference to a sign-off, a review log, a completeness checklist, or any documentation that would demonstrate the control was performed.
The description fails four of five elements outright and partially addresses only one (What). Per the NFRM control testing criteria, a control description must meet documentation criteria such that a knowledgeable person understands the control; this description does not meet that standard.

6. Verdict
Does Not Mitigate. The control description is a condition statement asserting a desired end-state rather than describing a control activity with an identifiable actor, action, trigger, and evidence. It provides no operational mechanism to detect or prevent the three failure modes in the stated risk (non-existence, incompleteness, or non-compliance of policies, standards, and functional rules).

7. Suggested Refinements
The following refinements are structured around the 4W+1H elements found Absent or Partially Stated in the Specificity Assessment. These address the description, not the underlying business process.

What: Bound the scope of documentation to the specific audited function — e.g., "all policies, standards, functional rules, and process descriptions governing agent network management, including IFA management." Specify what constitutes the complete document set (e.g., a defined policy register or catalogue).
Why: State the risk linkage explicitly — e.g., "to ensure that the governing framework for agent network management is complete, current, and compliant with group-level and regulatory requirements."
Who: Name the responsible actor or role — e.g., "the Agent Network Management function head" or "the designated policy owner, with oversight by [named committee or second-line function]."
When: Specify the frequency or trigger — e.g., "at least annually, and upon any material change in group requirements, regulatory requirements, or business model" — with a defined completion deadline.
How: Describe the verification method and the evidence generated — e.g., "by performing a completeness and compliance check of the policy register against the applicable NFRM catalogue and regulatory requirements, documented via a signed-off gap-analysis report and tracked in [named system]." Include the communication/training element from the full SAM Gov_3b definition: proper communication of the documented structure to all affected staff, evidenced by training records or acknowledgment logs.
8. Open Questions for the Auditor
Is a local operationalized control description available for Gov 3b that adds specificity beyond the SAM catalogue text? The description provided mirrors the SAM Annex A catalogue wording, which is written as a control objective rather than an operationalized control activity. If a local RACM or NFRM control sheet exists with a more detailed description, the Gate 1 assessment should be re-performed against that description. If no such operationalization exists, this is itself a finding (the governance control has not been translated into an assessable control activity at the OE level). Verdict impact if answered unfavorably: the verdict remains Does Not Mitigate, and the gap would be confirmed as a design deficiency.

Is the omission of the communication/training element intentional? The full SAM Gov_3b definition includes "and proper communication of the structure to all affected staff (including training sessions)." The provided description omits this. If intentional (e.g., covered by a separate control), confirm which control addresses the communication/training component. If unintentional, the omission represents a further scope gap. Verdict impact if answered unfavorably: the verdict does not worsen (already Does Not Mitigate), but the scope gap widens, requiring a more extensive refinement.

What specific policies, standards, and functional rules are expected to exist for agent network management / IFA management? A defined expected-document set (e.g., from the NFRM catalogue or local policy register) is needed to assess whether "up-to-date documentation" can even be evaluated for completeness. Verdict impact if answered unfavorably: without a defined baseline, neither this assessment nor a future DET/OET can evaluate completeness, reinforcing the need to operationalize the control description.

9. Methodology Compliance and References
Per the Allianz Standard for Internal Audit (SAM) V8.0, Annex A: Gov_3b is defined as "Operational structure documentation and Procedural knowledge/content expertise: Up-to-date documentation of operational structure (e.g., Corporate Rules/Policies, Adonis/Process descriptions, etc.) and proper communication of the structure to all affected staff (including training sessions)." The control description provided reproduces part of this catalogue definition but omits the communication/training element.

Per the SAM V6.0, Section B.III.2.a: "the adequacy of the control design (test of design) must be assessed to ensure it is suitable to adequately mitigate the underlying risk and in compliance with internal and external requirements; test of design includes the understanding of processes, interfaces and relevant controls."

Per the NFRM Control Testing guidance, the Test of Design assessment criteria include: "Meets control documentation criteria, such that a knowledgeable person understands"; "Control is performed by appropriate individuals"; "Sufficient evidence of control performance exists"; "Frequency of the control is sufficient." The control description as written does not satisfy these criteria.

Per the NFRM Functional Rule, Section C.IV.2: a control description should specify what is monitored, why the control is necessary, who performs or owns it, when and how frequently it is carried out, and how it is carried out including the evidence generated. The control description satisfies only one of five elements partially (What).

The uploaded methodology does not define an explicit Gate 1 pass/fail framework distinct from the Test of Design criteria above. The Gate 1 assessment structure used in this review (threshold test, failure-pathway mapping, 4W+1H specificity assessment) draws on the SAM and NFRM criteria cited above, supplemented by general IA practice (IIA Standards, risk-based auditing principles) for the structured reasoning framework. This reliance on general practice is clearly labeled.

10. Gate Status
Gate 1: Not Passed. The control description is a condition statement that does not constitute an assessable control activity and does not mitigate the stated risk. The description requires operationalization with a named actor, defined action, frequency/trigger, verification method, and evidence type before it can proceed to further assessment. Gate 2 (DET/OET review) is not yet available in this tool.
